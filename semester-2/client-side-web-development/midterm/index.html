Nike

Nike, Inc. is an American multinational corporation engaged in the design, development, manufacturing and marketing of sports equipment: balls, footwear, seeds, clothing, archery equipment, accessories and other sporting goods.

Products

  • Nike Air Max 270 – Known for its large air cushioning unit in the heel, offering comfort and style.
  • Nike Air Force 1 – A classic basketball-inspired shoe with a sleek, versatile design.
  • Nike ZoomX Vaporfly Next% – A high-performance running shoe designed for speed and efficiency, popular with marathon runners.
  • Nike Dunk Low – A retro basketball silhouette that’s become a streetwear staple.
  • Nike React Infinity Run Flyknit – A running shoe designed to offer a smooth and cushioned ride, with a lightweight feel.

Mission

Nike's mission is to bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete in the world. They believe that if you have a body, you're an athlete, and their goal is to create products that help people push their limits and achieve greatness.
